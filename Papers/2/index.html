A literature review of service robotics: Challenges and open research issues

Authors: A. Hentout, A. Maoudj & A. Kouider

Workshop paper: Workshop on Human-Robot Interaction: From Service to Industry (HRI-SI), 2022.

Publication record · Copy BibTeX

Abstract

Service robots have already made their way into people daily life; therefore, a great deal of research works has been proposed in the literature to improve their level in assisting humans in their activities of daily living. However, there are many challenges and open research issues for highly developed service robotics that still need to be addressed. Accordingly, this review paper provides an overview of the state-of-the-art of service robotics, discusses the major challenges and explores future research axes in this field.
