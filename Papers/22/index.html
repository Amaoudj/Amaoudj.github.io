Human–robot interaction in industrial collaborative robotics: a literature review of the decade 2008–2017

Authors: A. Hentout, M. Aouache, A. Maoudj & I. Akli

Published: Advanced Robotics, vol. 33, no. 15–16, pp. 764–799, 2019.

Publisher / DOI · Copy BibTeX

Abstract

Currently, a large number of industrial robots have been deployed to replace or assist humans to perform various repetitive and dangerous manufacturing tasks. However, based on current technological capabilities, such robotics field is rapidly evolving so that humans are not only sharing the same workspace with robots, but also are using robots as useful assistants. Consequently, due to this new type of emerging robotic systems, industrial collaborative robots or cobots, human and robot co-workers have been able to work side-by-side as collaborators to accomplish tasks in industrial environments.

Therefore, new human–robot interaction systems have been developed for such systems to be able to utilize the capabilities of both humans and robots.
