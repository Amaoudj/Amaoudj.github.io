Multi-agent architecture for telerobotics of heterogeneous multi-robot cooperation through negotiated task allocation with constraints: Initial implementation

Authors: A. Maoudj, B. Bouzouia, A. Hentout & R. Toumi

Published: 1re Journée d’Étude en Automatique et ses Applications (1JEAA14), pp. 38–43, 2014.

Publication record · Copy BibTeX

Abstract

This paper describes a multi-agent architecture for telerobotic control of heterogeneous multi-robot systems. The architecture is partially inspired from the bidding mechanisms to solve the problem of tasks planning and allocation. It is about a negotiation process between the agents of the architecture based on the Contract-Net Protocol.

It consists of allocating the task to the most suited robot which gives the best proposition. This latter encapsulates individual capacities to achieve the task with a quality guaranteed by the resources of each agent, and even the opportunity cost of achieving other tasks. Then, a local scheduling of the allocated tasks is carried out by the agents of the robots while minimizing their inoccupation times to converge rapidly towards an effective assignment of the tasks while minimizing the total execution time.
