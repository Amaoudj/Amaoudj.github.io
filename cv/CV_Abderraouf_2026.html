Research Profile

My work builds intelligent robot brains that reason on physical time and act safely on physical robots, with embodied AI for robot arms, legged robots and robot teams as the main application. For more than a decade I have carried out academic and industrial research and development in Algeria, Denmark and Saudi Arabia, with industrial partners that include Siemens, Mobile Industrial Robots and Enabled Robotics. The applications lie in intelligent manufacturing and smart logistics, and in embodied intelligence and the low-altitude economy, which China has named among its future and strategic emerging industries. One question organises the research: how can robots learn useful behaviour from limited interaction, keep working safely when their hardware or their task changes, and coordinate at industrial scale?

  • Embodied AI and world models: NeuroAct, a robot brain designed to keep working when the body or the task changes, whose shared model generalises in simulation to robot bodies it never saw in training; multi-agent world models (current work), in which each robot is curious about how its own actions and those of its teammates change the environment, so that the team learns a joint model of how the whole system evolves; and, as directions I am developing, vision-language-action policies that consult a world model before contact, and policies that reason on physical time.
  • Robot learning for intelligent manufacturing: as Principal Investigator of KFUPM Grant No. INMR2403, which I proposed, won and delivered, I built a dual-arm Meca500 skill-learning testbed that is now shared laboratory infrastructure in active use. On it, a decentralised policy per arm with graph attention between arms runs on the physical arms and coordinates more arms in simulation than it was trained with. First-author papers on multi-arm reinforcement learning and dual-arm manipulation are in press.
  • Multi-robot systems for smart logistics and drone teams: decentralised path finding and scheduling for warehouse robot fleets under limited communication, developed with the industrial partner Enabled Robotics, which coordinate very large fleets in simulation; and decentralised multi-drone gas-source localisation with a safety filter, which found the source far more often than infotaxis and model-predictive-control baselines (first author, Scientific Reports, accepted 2026, simulation).
  • Fault-tolerant legged robots and actuation: a co-authored controller in which reinforcement learning modulates central pattern generators was deployed zero-shot on a physical 18-DoF hexapod and kept it walking without a fall across repeated hardware trials, even with multiple legs disabled (under review); the open-source SPINDER hexapod platform (Scientific Reports, 2026); and, as Co-Principal Investigator of KFUPM Grant No. INMR2632, a dual-mode actuation joint for an open bipedal platform.

Research and Development Projects

Next-Generation Robotic Manipulation and Learning Systems

KFUPM · Ongoing · Postdoctoral Research Fellow

Learning-based manipulation for flexible industrial automation, combining reinforcement learning, imitation learning and inter-arm attention. A graph-attention SAC policy trained with up to four arms coordinates up to ten in simulation and runs on physical Meca500 arms. Current work explores multi-agent world models for sparse-reward coordination and vision–language–action policies for manipulation.

Laboratory demonstrations · Multi-arm planning video

NeuroAct: Transferable Robot Intelligence Across Bodies and Tasks

KFUPM · Ongoing · Postdoctoral Research Fellow

A modular control stack with an embodiment representation, hierarchical policies and persistent memory, designed to reduce retraining for new robots and tasks. A shared model has generalized to unseen synthetic robot morphologies in simulation; physical deployment is the next stage.

Resilient Legged Locomotion Under Progressive Hardware Failures

KFUPM · Ongoing · Postdoctoral Research Fellow

CPG dynamics modulated by reinforcement learning sustain locomotion as multiple joints and legs fail. A single policy trained with randomized fault injection was deployed zero-shot on a physical 18-DoF hexapod, supporting research toward dependable inspection robots. Companion work couples terrain perception and gait adaptation in quadruped simulation.

SPINDER demonstration · Open platform and simulator

Intelligent Bipedal Robotic Platform

KFUPM · Grant INMR2632 · Ongoing · Co-Principal Investigator

Open, modular hardware for reproducible locomotion, balance recovery, jumping and obstacle-negotiation experiments. Replaceable mechanical, sensing and control modules, standardized interfaces and synchronized logging support quantitative comparison of classical and learning-based controllers.

Collaborative Robotics for Industry 4.0

KFUPM · Grant INMR2403 · 2023–2025 · Principal Investigator

Developed multi-cobot skill learning, coordination and motion planning for flexible production, alongside fleet coordination for storage, transport and shipment preparation. Delivered a dual-arm Meca500 testbed with an integrated ROS stack, now used as shared research and teaching infrastructure.

Project record

Swarm Robotics for Industry 4.0

SDU Biorobotics · 2021–2023 · Postdoctoral Researcher
