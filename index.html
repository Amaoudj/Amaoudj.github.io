Dual-arm grocery picking for Saudi retail

A dual-arm mobile robot collects grocery orders in a simulated supermarket with shoppers and crowded aisles. Model-based planning and arm coordination work alongside learned dynamics compensation, exploring more adaptable order fulfilment for Saudi retail.

Shared-cell assembly

Coordination when it matters

Robot arms assemble parts from NIST Assembly Task Board 1 in a shared workspace. The research explores how to coordinate when tasks overlap while allowing independent motion elsewhere, with additional experiments on physical industrial arms.

Cooperative aerial inspection

Drone teams that search for gas leaks

Three drones share source-location estimates and coordinate their search through changing gas readings. Multi-agent reinforcement learning guides motion, while a safety filter adjusts commands for separation and obstacle clearance. The associated paper is accepted in Scientific Reports.

MuJoCo simulation · Camera-only tracking
Distributed visual sensing

A drone team that chooses who looks

Six camera drones track a target that shares no position telemetry, with only three detectors active at a time. An explicit planner selects complementary views and positions standby drones to reduce tracking gaps, with potential applications around industrial sites and emergency-response operations.

A connected research agenda

Learning, embodiment & cooperation

01

Robot learning & manipulation

Learning skills from interaction and demonstration; combining reinforcement learning, world models, and vision–language–action policies with reliable multi-arm planning and control.

02

Resilient, embodied intelligence

Bio-inspired control and learning for legged robots that adapt to terrain and hardware faults. Open robot platforms connect mechanism design, control, and sim-to-real experimentation.

03

Coordinated robot teams

Decentralized path finding, task allocation, and cooperative perception for robot fleets in warehouses and industrial environments, where local decisions must support effective teamwork.
